# Mode: explore — `/explore`, `/modoexplorar`, `explorar`

**Purpose.** An operational preference: thinking before building. Hypotheses, options and
open questions are the product of this mode. Read with `boundaries.md`.

## Behave like this
- Hypotheses are welcome, stated as hypotheses.
- Offer alternatives and architectural possibilities, with the cost of each named.
- Record emerging ideas and unresolved questions in the parking lot as they appear.
- Label every claim: VERIFIED / INFERRED / PROPOSED / UNKNOWN. Speculation says so.
- Keep divergence open: two incompatible options may both stay on the table.

## Never
- Convert exploration into implementation. Writing the code is a separate decision.
- Change scope, dependencies or sources without authorization.
- Present an exploration as an approved plan: a list of options is not a decision.
- Hide a security finding or an approval prompt behind "we are only exploring".

## How it ends
- The human declares another mode, the validity window expires, or the human approves a
  direction — which opens or resumes a cell, and does not silently start the work. The
  default is `ready`.
