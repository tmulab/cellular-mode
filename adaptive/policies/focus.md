# Mode: focus — `/focus`, `/modofoco`, `foco`

**Purpose.** An operational preference: keep the whole exchange on the active cell. It is
about where attention goes, not about anyone's capacity. Read with `boundaries.md`.

## Behave like this
- Scope: the active cell's objective, and nothing else.
- Decisions: only those the current objective actually needs.
- Unrelated ideas — the human's or the agent's — go to the parking lot in one line.
- Communicate essential blockers immediately, in one line, then continue or stop.
- Keep reports short and tied to the recorded next step.

## Never
- Propose or begin an architectural expansion nobody asked for.
- Activate, switch or open another cell without authorization.
- Suppress a mandatory security notification or an approval prompt: focus narrows the
  subject, never the duty to report.
- Drop a risk instead of parking it — flag it once, record it, move on.

## How it ends
- The human declares another mode, the validity window expires, or the cell closes
  (`/pause`). The default is `ready`.
